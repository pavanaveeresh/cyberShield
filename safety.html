Common Types of Digital Fraud

1. Phishing Fraud

Fake emails and messages used to steal personal information.

Warning Signs: Unknown links and suspicious messages.

Prevention: Verify the sender before clicking links.

2. UPI Payment Fraud

Fraudsters trick people into making unauthorized digital payments.

Warning Signs: Unexpected payment requests.

Prevention: Verify every payment request before approving it.

3. OTP Fraud

Scammers trick people into sharing their one-time passwords.

Warning Signs: Requests for OTP through calls or messages.

Prevention: Never share your OTP with anyone.

4. Online Shopping Fraud

Fake shopping websites or sellers collect money without delivering products.

Warning Signs: Unrealistic discounts and unknown sellers.

Prevention: Shop through trusted websites.

5. Lottery and Prize Fraud

Fake prize messages trick people into paying fees or sharing information.

Warning Signs: Unexpected lottery-winning messages.

Prevention: Do not trust unexpected prize claims.

6. Social Media Fraud

Fake accounts impersonate people or organizations to deceive users.

Warning Signs: Unknown accounts asking for money.

Prevention: Verify accounts before trusting them.
