Know Where and How to Report Online Fraud

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

Government of India provides an official portal to report cybercrime incidents.

Visit Official Portal

Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930

Call 1930 immediately to report financial cyber fraud.

Steps to Report Cyber Fraud

1. Act Quickly

Report financial fraud immediately through helpline 1930.

2. Collect Evidence

Keep transaction details, screenshots, messages and relevant information.

3. Visit Official Portal

Open the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

4. Submit Complaint

Follow the official portal instructions and provide accurate information.

5. Save Acknowledgement

Keep your complaint acknowledgement number for future reference.

Important Safety Advice

Never share OTP, PIN, passwords or banking details with unknown people.

Contact your bank through its official channels if your account is affected.
