Our Project

CyberShield is a cyber safety and digital fraud awareness project designed to educate users about online threats and safe internet practices.

Our Objectives

  • Spread awareness about common digital frauds.
  • Educate users about online safety.
  • Identify common warning signs in suspicious messages.
  • Help users understand how to report cybercrime.

Technologies Used

HTML – Creates the structure of the website.

CSS – Designs and styles the website.

JavaScript – Adds interactive features.
